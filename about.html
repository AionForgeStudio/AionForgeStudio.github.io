About

A studio that looks like its work

AionForge is an independent game studio focused on Minecraft packs, playable worlds, creature systems, and the creative tools that make production faster without lowering the bar.

Our visual language comes straight from the workbench: legendary weapons like Trophy Edge, mech midbosses like Chrono Robo Sentinel, and apex creatures like Triassic Relic Rex — dense cube models with honest materials and focal energy.

What guides the work

  • Readable first — silhouettes and materials players can parse instantly.
  • Production quality — animation-ready structure, not throwaway demos.
  • Honest status — public pages stay current with real project state.
  • Bedrock-minded — packs and systems built for real runtime constraints.

Source and studio projects live under github.com/AionForgeStudio. Need a human? Visit the contact page.
